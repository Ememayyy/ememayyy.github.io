HOW IT WORKS

Momentum is not your official rating.

Daily Momentum is an experimental recent-form index. It weighs the difficulty of accepted problems, unique solves, active days, rated contests and Codeforces contribution to estimate the competitive level your recent activity resembles.

Recent form + Difficulty + Consistency + Contest form + Momentum Rank
This is a community-style metric, not an official Codeforces rating or ranking. Very active users may exceed the first 1,000 recent submissions available to this lightweight frontend analyzer.
